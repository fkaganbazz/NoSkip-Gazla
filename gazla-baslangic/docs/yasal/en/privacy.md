# NoSkip Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains what data NoSkip (called Gazla in Turkish; the "App") collects, why, who it's shared with, and your
rights. Data controller: [PUBLISHER], [ADDRESS], [SUPPORT EMAIL].

In short: we collect only what the App needs to work and we don't sell your data. Your photo proofs are visible only to
your friends in that challenge. The email addresses in your contacts never reach our servers. You can delete your account
from inside the App.

## 1. Data we collect

**Account and profile**
- The email address from your sign-in method (with Sign in with Apple this may be Apple's private relay address) and the
  name Apple shares on first sign-in (optional, used only as a name suggestion).
- Your username, display name, avatar color and optional profile photo.
- Your birth year: only to enforce the minimum age (13).
- The date you accepted the terms.

**Settings**
- Time zone and language (so days are counted correctly and notifications arrive at the right time and in the right
  language), notification and reminder preferences, the "who can find me" choice, late-night grace, roast mode.

**Data created while you use the App**
- Challenges (name, duration, start date, reminder time, invite message, who can see it), memberships and invites.
- Check-ins: the day, a number value, optional photo proof and note. Streaks, saves, badges.
- Pokes (the ID of a ready-made message; there's no free text), friendships and requests, blocks.
- Reports (the reason you pick and the description you write) and in-app notifications.

**Contacts (only if you tap "Find from contacts" and allow access)**
- Your contacts' email addresses are turned into one-way hashes (SHA-256) on your phone; only the hashes are sent to our
  server and compared with users who chose "anyone can find me". The hashes are not stored; to prevent abuse we keep only
  the number and time of lookups.
- A hash of your own email address is stored so friends can find you. If you choose "Nobody" in Settings, you won't appear
  in contact lookups.

**Device**
- Your device's notification token (push token) and platform (iOS/Android), to send notifications. Deleted when you sign
  out.
- Some preferences, like theme and language, are stored only on your device.

**Ads**
- Ads are served by Google AdMob. If you allow personalized ads (the consent form in the EU, "Allow tracking" on iOS),
  Google may use your advertising ID; if you don't, you'll see non-personalized ads. Google processes data such as IP
  address and device information under its own policy to serve ads.
- When you save a streak with a rewarded ad, Google sends our server your user ID and the challenge/day being saved so we
  can verify the reward is real.

**Error reports**
- If the App crashes or hits an error, a technical report is sent to Sentry: error details, device model, operating
  system, app version and the screen you were on. We don't send your name, email or content; IP addresses are not stored.

The App does not collect your location, microphone, or the names/phone numbers in your contacts. It accesses the camera
and photos only when you take or pick a photo.

## 2. Why we use data
| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Creating your account; running challenges, streaks and friendships | Performance of contract (Terms of Use) |
| Sending notifications and reminders | Device permission; you can turn them off in Settings |
| Finding friends from contacts | Your consent (permission and the "Find from contacts" action) |
| Personalized ads | Your consent; you can withdraw it at any time |
| Verifying rewarded ads, preventing abuse and fraud, reviewing reports | Legitimate interest, legal obligation |
| Finding and fixing bugs | Legitimate interest |

We don't sell your data or build profiles to hand to third parties.

## 3. Who sees it
- **Other users:** your display name, username, profile photo and avatar color are visible to your friends, to people in
  shared challenges, and to people who search for you (depending on "who can find me"). Your check-in status, streak and
  rank in a challenge are visible to its members; your photo proofs and notes only to members of that challenge. People
  you block can't see you.
- **Our service providers** (only to provide the service, under contract):
  - Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage; servers in [REGION])
  - Google (AdMob ads, Sign in with Google)
  - Apple (Sign in with Apple, iOS notifications)
  - Expo / 650 Industries (notification delivery)
  - Sentry (error reports)
  - An email delivery service (sign-in link emails)
- **Legal requirements:** in response to a lawful request from competent authorities.

Some of these providers are in the United States, so your data may be transferred outside Türkiye and the EU. Transfers
are made with safeguards required by applicable law (standard contractual clauses, consent).

## 4. How long we keep it
- As long as your account is open.
- When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), your profile, content, photos, notification tokens and all
  related records are deleted immediately. They drop out of backups within [7] days at the latest. Records we're legally
  required to keep (if any) are kept only for that period.
- Error reports are kept in Sentry for at most [90] days.

## 5. Security
Connections are encrypted (TLS). In the database each user can only see the rows they're allowed to see (row-level
security). Photo proofs are not public; they're shown through short-lived signed links.

## 6. Children
The App is not for children under 13. If we learn that someone under 13 has created an account, we'll delete it.

## 7. Your rights
You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to object to processing, to receive your data in a portable
format, and to withdraw consent. You can edit your profile and delete your account in the App; for anything else write to
[SUPPORT EMAIL] and we'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Türkiye,
the Personal Data Protection Authority).

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy. We'll announce important changes in the App or by email.
