# NoSkip Help

## Getting started

**What is NoSkip?**
An app for doing 7, 10 or 30-day challenges with your friends. Check in every day, keep the streak, poke the slackers.
Diken is watching.

**How do I start a challenge?**
Tap + in the bottom bar. Pick a ready-made template or create your own: set the name, duration, task type (done, photo,
number) and reminder time, then invite your friends.

**How do I add friends?**
In the Friends tab, search by username, use "Find from contacts", or share your invite link. If the App isn't installed,
the link opens the download page; if it is, it goes straight to the invite.

## Days and streaks

**When does a day end?**
At midnight in your time zone. Night owls can pick 0, 1 or 2 hours of late-night grace in Settings (default 2 hours):
during that time you can still check in for yesterday.

**How is the streak counted?**
Check in every day and your streak grows. Miss a day and it breaks. It's calculated on our servers; changing your time
zone doesn't reopen past days.

**My streak broke. What now?**
You can save it within 24 hours: watch a short ad or use your one free save of the month. A saved day's next day can't be
saved.

**What if I fall short on a number task?**
The day still counts as checked in and your streak stays alive.

## Pokes

**What's a poke?**
Send a friend a ready-made message: "Hype up" (encouragement) or "Roast" (teasing). There's no free text.

**Why can't I roast someone?**
Roasts only work if the other person turned on roast mode in Settings.

**How many times can I poke?**
Up to 3 times a day per person. You can undo within 30 seconds; an undone poke still counts toward the daily limit.

## Notifications

**Which notifications will I get?**
A daily reminder at your reminder time, a heads-up before the day ends, a note when your streak breaks; plus pokes,
requests, invites and "finished" notifications from friends. Diken sends at most 2 notifications a day and stays quiet
from 23:30 to 08:00.

**How do I turn notifications off?**
In Settings → Notifications you can turn off poke notifications and reminders separately. To turn everything off, use
your phone's settings.

## Privacy and safety

**Who can see my photo proofs?**
Only your friends in that challenge.

**Who can find me?**
If "Anyone can find me" is on in Settings, people can find you by username and from their contacts. If it's off, you can
only be added with an invite link.

**How do I block or report someone?**
From the "…" menu on their profile or next to the photo. Someone you block can't find you or send you pokes or requests.
We review reports within 24 hours.

## Account

**How do I change the language?**
Settings → Language. With "System", your phone's language is used.

**How do I delete my account?**
Settings → Delete account. Your profile, challenges, photos and all your data are permanently deleted; this can't be
undone.

**What if I get a new phone?**
Sign in the same way (Apple, Google or email) and your streak, garden and friends come with you.
